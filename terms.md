1.3 These Terms sit beside the Tomty Tester Privacy Policy (the "Privacy Policy") at https://snune002.github.io/tomty-legal/privacy.

1.4 You agree to these Terms by ticking "I agree to the Tester Terms and Privacy Policy, and to my voice and lists being processed by the artificial intelligence services listed in the Privacy Policy."
and tapping Continue when you enter your enrollment code in the app. You must be 18 or older.

## 2. What to expect from a test build

2.1 The app is unfinished. It may have bugs, change, or stop working. Material changes follow section 7.

2.2 Your lists live on your phone. Test builds can lose data. Do not rely on the app for anything important.

2.3 You use the app at your own risk. We give no promise that it will work or stay available.

## 3. Daily and weekly limits

3.1 To keep costs in check, each tester has an allowance for the costly features.

3.2 **Voice:** 10 minutes of recorded speech a day and 30 minutes a week. The day resets at midnight UTC. The
week starts on Monday at midnight UTC.

3.3 **Content:** 50 MB of downloaded content a day. The day resets at midnight UTC.

These are maximum allowances. A shared test limit can pause a feature for everyone before you reach your own limit.

3.4 Typed sorting, store search, maps and store data have daily limits that sit well above normal use. If one
device uses far more than its fair share of a day, the app pauses that feature on that device until the next
day.

3.5 Voice and store search show a message when a limit is reached. Some features, such as automatic item sorting, fall
back without a message. Recordings waiting for transcription stay on your phone and can resume when the limit clears
and the app is open. A command already turned into text may remain as a draft for you to Process.

3.6 We may change the limits.

## 4. Pausing the app

4.1 We may pause or stop the app, or one device, for heavy use, for misuse, or for any other reason. A paused
device sees a message and a contact email.

4.2 We may end the test at any time.

## 5. What you agree to

5.1 You will not try to get around the limits, the enrollment code or the app's security.

5.2 Do not share your enrollment code. Tester codes work for one phone.

5.3 You will not use the app to break the law or to harm others.

5.4 You will not copy the app, take it apart or use its content outside the app.

## 6. AI processing

6.1 To work, the app sends your voice recordings and your list names and items, and may send your food preferences and allergies, to outside artificial intelligence services. The Privacy Policy names them.

6.2 You agree to this with the same tick that accepts these Terms at enrollment (clause 1.4) and whenever you agree again (section 7).

6.3 If you agree, we may use the words of your voice commands to develop and improve our own model, as the Privacy Policy, clause 3.20, "Developing our own model", describes. You can withdraw at any time, as the Privacy Policy explains.

## 7. Changes to these Terms

7.1 If we make a material change to the Tester Terms or Privacy Policy, the app tells you when it next connects to our servers. You must agree to the updated documents before using online features again.

7.2 Small corrections, such as typos and clearer wording, need nothing from you.

7.3 The app records which version you agreed to, and when.

## 8. Stopping

8.1 You can stop at any time. In Profile, "Stop using Tomty's online services" withdraws your agreement.
"Delete my device data" removes what our server holds about your device.

8.2 The Privacy Policy says what deletion does and does not remove.
